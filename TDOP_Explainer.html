TDOP is DFW Terminal D's live operations briefing tool. It pulls flight data directly from the Stand & Gate API, displays Terminal D and F-Hardstand ops side-by-side, tracks real-time gate changes, and sends formatted briefings and live Teams alerts to every ops team — all in one browser tab.

Toolbar Buttons

⟳ Refresh

Pulls live data for the selected date & shift window. Your first action. Also re-pulls automatically every 5 minutes. 🔒 Date unlocks the date fields.

💾 / ↓

Save writes your inputs to the cloud; Load restores them. Auto-saves every 30 min and syncs between machines every 10 sec.

✉ All Ops

Send Report ▾ menu — full Terminal D briefing email to all ops lists.

HS Ops

Send Report ▾ — hardstand-only brief to Gate Control / HS leadership.

LF Ops

Send Report ▾ — Frontier (F9) scoped brief to the Menzies F9 ops team.

Intl Ops

Send Report ▾ — email plus a Teams card for international ops.

📋 Traffic

Send Report ▾ — arrivals list for the current From / To time window shown in the header.

F9 RONs

Send Report ▾ — Frontier remain-overnight flights for the window.

SY AM Lineup

Send Report ▾ — Sun Country AM lineup email.

NightBrief

Send Report ▾ — opens the NightBrief page in a new tab.

👁 SeeSay

Posts a Teams card in the Bussing chat telling staff to use the SeeSay app for IOC requests. Always enabled.

🚌 Bus Needed

Popup: enter Airline, From and To — posts a Bus Needed card to Teams with the time.

🚨 Alerts ▾

Alert I / Alert II open a form (runway, callsign, A/C type, issue, FOB, SOB, ETA) and post to Teams. Alert Terminated posts an all-clear.

🔧 EQUIP

Opens the equipment move / removal email form.

📋 Reference

Menu: Quick Start Guide, Help (this User Guide) and Phonebook. ☀️/🌙 toggles light / dark mode.

🗑 Clear All

Wipes saved state, ETAs, notes and flags. Use at end of shift.

Airline Card Anatomy

Qatar Airways · QR
⚠ Gate Changed GATED
ARR QR 739 D-16 STA 14:35 ETA 14:52
DEP QR 740 D-16 STD 16:15 pax: 289
①

Header bar — airline name + IATA. Amber ⚠ when gate/pad changed; green GATED when on hold.

②

ARR / DEP rows — flight number, gate, STA/STD (crossed out when ETA entered), and ETA/ETD in orange.

③

Pax field — enter expected pax count per leg. Carries into all exports.

④

✗ Cancel checkbox — strikes the leg red. Both legs cancelled → card moves to terminal section.

⑤

Note field — per-leg free text (up to 100 chars). Appears in emails and Teams posts.

Shift Start Workflow

1

Open TDOP. Choose Load Saved to continue today's shared state, or New Briefing to start clean.

2

Set the From / To date & time in the header. Tap 🔒 Date to unlock a locked date.

3

Click ⟳ Refresh. Status dot turns green; hardstand (left) and terminal (right) cards populate.

4

Enter ETAs / ETDs, pax and notes per leg. ETA/ETD pre-fill from the API; anything you type wins. Check ✗ to cancel a leg.

5

Save with 💾 when you make key changes (it also auto-saves every 30 min). Other machines pick it up within 10 seconds.

6

Send Briefings from ✉ Send Report ▾; use 🚌 Bus Needed, 🚨 Alerts or 👁 SeeSay for live Teams posts.

7

End of shift: click 🗑 Clear All so the incoming coordinator starts clean. The app also resets itself at 3:00 AM.

Key Features

⚠️
Gate/Pad Changed badge — amber flag when the API detects a reassignment since the first pull. Hover the highlighted cell to see the original value.
☁️
Cloud save & live sync — Save / Load use shared cloud storage, so any machine or browser sees the same briefing. Auto-save runs every 30 min and other machines update every 10 sec.
🔄
Auto-refresh — flight data re-pulls every 5 minutes without losing anything you typed.
🚌
BusBoard — the hardstand snapshot is published automatically after every successful refresh for bus drivers. No button needed.
🚨
Alerts — Alert I and II need at least one field filled; blanks show as N/A on the card. Alert Terminated posts instantly with no form.
🚌
Bus Needed — Airline, From and To are all required. The Teams card shows the post time.
📋
Traffic — follows whatever From / To times are in the header, including windows that cross midnight.
✅
Selecting cards — tick two or more airline cards to get ⎘ Email and 📤 Post to Teams for just those flights. Use + Add to enter a flight manually.
🔴
+1 Next-Day badge — marks times that cross midnight so you know it is the following calendar day.
🔓
Date lock — the date locks after a pull to prevent accidents. Tap 🔒 Date to change it.
⏰
Nightly reset — at 3:00 AM the saved state is wiped and a fresh briefing is pulled. Gate-change flags start fresh each day.
